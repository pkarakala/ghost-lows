# Spec review (2026-10-18)

A critical review of `docs/` made before any code was written, so that problems in the specs are fixed before the phase that depends on them. Each item lists the evidence, a proposed fix and when it must be resolved. **Status** is `open` until the spec is changed or the item is rejected. Record the decision and commit here.

Evidence sources checked on 2026-10-18: PubMed abstracts of [Facchinetti 2016], [Baysal 2014], [Lobo 2026], [Idi 2024], [Manzoni 2025], [Basu 2013] and Bergenstal 2018 (GMI); the full text of [Baysal 2014] (PMC4455457); the simglucose 0.2.11 wheel source; the GlucoBench archive at commit `661d840` (profiled); the DiaData v3 Zenodo record and README.

Items marked **PI decision** change the study design or a research question. They were decided on 2026-10-18 under delegated authority; see [`DECISIONS.md`](DECISIONS.md) (D1–D18) for the decisions and the pilot evidence behind them. The others are corrections to make in the phase that implements them.

## Contradicted by a source (fix before the phase)

| ID | Phase | Issue and evidence | Proposed fix | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SR1 | 2 | **Wrong truth compartment.** simglucose's `BG` is `observation.Gsub` = state x[12]/Vg (subcutaneous), the same signal the CGM reads plus noise (`env.py`, `sensor/cgm.py`). The model already lags it behind plasma: `dx12 = ksc·(x3 − x12)`, τ = 1/ksc = 6.6–19.9 min across the 30 patients (median ≈ 11). SIMULATION §1's extra 5–10 min filter would count the lag twice. Basu 2013 reports 5.3–6.2 min (8 healthy fasted adults), so the 10 min upper bound is not from that source. | `truth_glucose` = plasma glucose x[3]/Vg, no extra filter. State as a limitation that the model's lag exceeds Basu's, so warning times are pessimistic. **PI decision.** | decided: D1 |
| SR2 | 3 | **Dropout model contradicts [Facchinetti 2016].** SIM §4.2 assumes log-normal gaps with a median of 20 min. Facchinetti 2016 models disconnections with a three-state Markov chain and reports ~90% last <20 min. | Use Facchinetti's Markov model (needs the full text); otherwise a median well below 20 min, cited. | interim model in SIM §4.2; full text pending |
| SR3 | 3 | **PISA time constant missing.** The abstract confirms "first-order linear dynamic system driven by a rectangular function" and 45 (30–70) min / 24 (10–48) mg/dL. These are properties of the *observed artifact*. Sampled as the rectangle's length and amplitude, they give shallower (peak = A(1−e^(−D/τ))) and longer artifacts. τ is not specified; it sets the initial fall rate A/τ, which decides whether Baysal's entry threshold fires. | Take τ and the input-to-artifact mapping from the full text. The Phase 3 test checks depth and duration measured on the generated trace. | interim τ = 10 min and realised-size mapping (SIM §4.1); full text pending |
| SR4 | 3 | **Log-normal cannot match the depth quantiles.** In log space the 5th/95th percentiles are 0.88/0.69 from the median. A symmetric fit gives 95th ≈ 53 vs 48 mg/dL. Duration fits (0.41/0.44). The depth–duration correlation is unknown. | 3-parameter or quantile-interpolated distribution, or an explicit tolerance. Mark independence of depth and duration as an `ASSUMPTION`. | decided: two-piece log-normal (SIM §4.1) |
| SR5 | 3 | **"1 PISA per 73 h" not in [Lobo 2026] abstract.** Abstract counts: 846/59,122 h (1 per 70 h), 89/8,189 h (1/92 h), 325/26,890 h (1/83 h); pooled 1/75 h. It counts only PISAs with minimum sensor glucose <85 mg/dL, so the rate depends on the population's overnight glucose level. | Check the full text (PMC13486385). Calibrate on the observed-below-85 rate and use Facchinetti 2016's frequency data as a second anchor. (CR3) | open: full text pending (CR3) |
| SR6 | 4 | **Retrospective detectors used as real-time gates.** [Idi 2024] is titled "Unsupervised *Retrospective* Detection…"; [Manzoni 2025] is retrospective; README calls [Idi 2023] retrospective. A causal matched filter is late by construction. | Name them `*_causal`, document deviations, report detection latency, and also score the retrospective originals on the retro track. | decided: D7 |
| SR7 | 3–4 | **Baysal thresholds are a parameter sweep.** [Baysal 2014] Table 1: g′_in ∈ {−1.90…−3.10} is the sweep range of the entry threshold (sets: aggressive −1.9, trial −2.0, nominal −2.5, cautious −3.1). It is not a constraint on real PISA drop rates (SIM §4.1). The method needs a Kalman estimate from Cameron et al. (not in CITATIONS), and was developed on Medtronic Enlite data with single-engineer, per-reading labels (CR4). | Pre-specify one parameter set (and sweep the others as a sensitivity analysis). Add Cameron et al. to CITATIONS. Drop the realism sentence from SIM §4.1. | decided: realism sentence replaced (SIM §4.1), one pre-set parameter set (BENCH §2); add Cameron et al. in Phase 4 |
| SR8 | 7 | **DiaData overlaps Weinstock and D1NAMO.** Its README lists Jaeb 537 (Severe Hypoglycemia in Older Adults = Weinstock 2016) and D1NAMO. The preprocessed release interpolates ≤120 min gaps and removes IQR outliers. | Deduplicate; use the raw release. (DATA.md updated.) | decided: DATA.md, SIM §5 |
| SR9 | 0 | **DATA.md facts.** Weinstock spans a median of 13.9 days, not ~10; 39/401 are censoring sentinels; it is enriched for lows. Dubosson has calories, not carb grams. The GlucoBench repo has no licence. | Fixed in DATA.md (commit `1756b27`). | resolved |
| SR10 | 1 | **GMI source.** The Phase 1 prompt cites [Battelino 2019] for GMI. The formula is from Bergenstal 2018, *Diabetes Care* 41:2275, doi:10.2337/dc18-1581. | Add Bergenstal 2018 to CITATIONS and the bib in the Phase 1 commit that implements GMI. | resolved (Phase 1) |
| SR11 | 0 | **Truncated DOI.** `references.bib` had Battelino 2023 as `10.1016/S2213-8587(22`. | Fixed to `10.1016/S2213-8587(22)00319-9`. | resolved |

## Threats to the validity of RQ2

| ID | Phase | Issue | Proposed fix | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SR12 | 2 | **Context from recorded data only.** COB must use *announced* carbs (miscounted, and still present when the meal is skipped). IOB must use *delivered* insulin. Never use simulator truth. simglucose's `BBController` boluses on true carbs, so a custom controller is needed. | State in SIM §2–3; add a test that context columns are computable from the logged events alone. | decided: SIM §2 |
| SR13 | 4–6 | **Time of night is not insulin/carb context.** Every CGM user has it, and PISA onset is drawn from *our* assumed window, so a time feature learns the assumption. | Factorial ablation: CGM-only, +time, +IOB/COB, +all. **PI decision.** | decided: D2 |
| SR14 | 2, 6 | **The context benefit is a dial we set.** The share of lows IOB cannot explain is fixed by scenario parameters (CR13). | Report the context benefit against an explicit "fraction of nocturnal lows not explained by IOB", at ≥3 levels. **PI decision.** | decided: D3 |
| SR15 | 3–6 | **Generator–detector coupling.** The matched-filter template and the ML training data come from the same generator as the test PISAs, which flatters template methods. | A robustness arm with a mismatched shape (τ ±50%, second-order or skewed shape, a different depth distribution). | decided: D8 |
| SR16 | 2, 7 | **Post-hoc fault injection needs an open loop.** BBController corrects only at meals when glucose >150, so overnight PISAs do not feed back (fine). An AID controller would invalidate post-hoc injection. In real AID data (AZT1D, Control-IQ) IOB reacts to the CGM, including PISAs. | Keep injection post-hoc only for open-loop controllers. In AZT1D, build context from user-entered boluses and carbs, or simulate the loop. Frame H3 accordingly. | decided: D9 |
| SR17 | 5 | **Cause attribution.** BENCHMARK §3's point-wise rule misses trend alarms that fire before CGM <70. It also mislabels CGM lag during recovery from a real low (CGM below truth) as noise. | Attribute at the alarm: fault active in [t−w, t]; add a "near-event/lag" cause; define precedence for overlapping faults. Re-check the "82% noise" motivation (SR25) under this rule. | decided: D10 |

## Vague or under-specified

| SR19 | 5 | **Matching.** Alarms after onset+15 during an ongoing low: false, late or ignored? One alarm in two windows?
