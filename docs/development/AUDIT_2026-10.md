# Audit (2026-10-27)

A hostile review of the code, tests and statistics at `v0.3.0` (`7bf1578`), after the [2026-10-18 review](REVIEW.md). Items that review already raised (P4–P9 in [DECISIONS.md](../reference/DECISIONS.md#proposed-amendments-not-adopted), real-data circularity, in-sample thresholds) are not repeated.

## Summary

**Verdict.** No finding changes a pre-registered result or its reading. Six bugs were fixed without changing any number in `results/`. Four findings would change numbers if fixed. They are reported here with draft amendments P10–P13 for the project lead to accept or reject.

**Method.**
- Read every module in `src/ghostlows/`, the experiment scripts, the tests and the specifications.
- Regenerated `results/README.md` from the committed tables: byte-identical.
- Ran `uv lock --check` (lock consistent) and the full test suite.
- Measured two effects on the Weinstock data and the PISA sampler (items 1 and 3).
- Every new test fails on `7bf1578` and passes after the fix.

**Fixed (no number in `results/` changes).**
- Learned alarms with no lows in training fired every 30 minutes (item 4).
- `low_events` counted readings either side of a gap as consecutive (item 5).
- An interrupted simulation could be reused without its event logs, and packaged that way (item 6).
- The D5 reference alarm ignored a configured refractory period (item 7).
- Run provenance did not record uncommitted changes (item 8).
- A wrong redraw share in a docstring (item 16).

**Reported, would change numbers.**
- Real-data reference lows do not follow the BENCHMARK.md §3 gap rule. Following it adds 5.6% level 1 and 5.5% level 2 Weinstock reference lows (P10).
- The τ = 20 robustness arm also lengthens PISAs: median 55 instead of 45 min (P12).
- H1's percentile CI for a median of 30 patients, and silently dropped bootstrap draws (P13).

**Reported, adds outputs only.** Five specified outputs were never produced (P11):
- detection latency;
- the retrospective track;
- the depth-mismatch arms;
- the effective sample size;
- the share of PISAs that overlap a meal bolus.

**Checked and sound.**
- **Causality.** The causality test covers every benchmark and stretch alarm and the plug-in example.
- **No leakage.** Folds keep patients whole and are stratified by age. Thresholds and normalisation use training patients only. No feature reads future samples.
- **Faults.** Faults never touch truth.
- **Pairing.** H2 and A13 bootstraps are paired over patients.
- **Pins.** CI installs the lock with `--locked`.

## Findings, most severe first

Severity:
- *Medium:* a measured deviation from the specification, or a bug with serious effects if triggered.
- *Low:* latent, or a reporting gap.
- *Info:* no action needed.

Line numbers are for this branch.

**1. Real-data reference lows ignore the gap rule.** Medium. Reported (P10).
- **Where.** `experiments/real_data.py:85-119` (`to_grid`, `reference_events`) with `src/ghostlows/bench/events.py:15` (`detect_events`).
- **Rule.** BENCHMARK.md §3 says that on real data, "a gap longer than two sampling intervals ends the run".
- **What the code does.** Missing readings become NaN on a 5-min grid, and NaN counts as "at or above".
  - So one missing reading splits a run of low readings.
  - An event ends only after three missing readings in a row; the rule ends it after two.
- **Evidence.** 27% of Weinstock grid slots are empty. As built, Weinstock has 2,764 level 1 and 1,550 level 2 reference lows (`real__meta.json`). Read as specified, it has 2,918 and 1,635: +154 (+5.6%) and +85 (+5.5%).
- **Effect if fixed.**
  - Every row of `real__summary.csv` changes.
  - The AZT1D tables (`real__azt1d_*`) probably change too. AZT1D was not measured: its data is not in this environment.
  - No reading is expected to flip, but this is unverified.

**2. Specified outputs never produced.** Medium. Reported (P11).
- **What is missing.** These are not in P9:
  - D7 detection latency for gated detectors (BENCHMARK.md:68).
  - D7's retrospective track for the retrospective originals (BENCHMARK.md:50).
  - D8's depth-quantile mismatch arms: only the τ arms exist (`experiments/configs/robust_tau*.yaml`).
  - The effective sample size for repeated seeds (BENCHMARK.md:86).
  - The share of PISAs overlapping a meal bolus (SIMULATION.md:58, 87).
- **Why it matters.** The last one bounds the assumption that injecting faults after simulation is harmless. A pre-registration reviewer checks outputs against the protocol.

**3. The τ = 20 arm changes PISA duration as well as τ.** Medium. Reported (P12).
- **Where.** `src/ghostlows/faults/pisa.py:111-121`. Durations shorter than τ·ln 10 + 1 min are redrawn.
- **Evidence.** 200,000 draws:

  | τ | Redrawn | Median duration | Shortest |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 5 min | 0% | 45 min | 14 min |
  | 10 min (nominal) | 0.6% | 45 min | 24 min |
  | 20 min | 57% | 55.5 min | 47 min |

- **Effect.** `robust_tau20` tests slower *and* longer PISAs. Its unchanged compression rate (0.33 vs 0.32 per day for `trend`) cannot be read as robustness to τ alone.

**4. A learned alarm with nothing to learn fired constantly.** Medium (latent). Fixed.
- **Where.** `src/ghostlows/alarms/learned.py:226,287`.
- **The bug.**
  - With no lows in training, `CombinedAlarm` and `GBMAlarm` scored 0.
  - The D5 candidates then reduce to one threshold just below 0 (`stats.py:68-79`).
  - So the alarm fired every refractory period, although the code comment says "never fires".
- **Fix.** The alarms now score −∞. Test: `test_learned_alarm_with_no_lows_in_training_never_fires`.
- **Effect on results.** None: every training fold has lows (`thresholds.csv`).

**5. `low_events` joined readings across gaps.** Medium (public API). Fixed.
- **Where.** `src/ghostlows/bench/events.py:68-120`.
- **The bug.** Readings were treated as consecutive rows. Two low readings, two hours without data and one more low reading made a 15-minute low.
- **Fix.** Readings now go on a regular grid at the median step, as in the real-data track. Test: `test_low_events_never_join_readings_across_a_gap`.
- **Effect on results.** None: the pipeline does not use `low_events`, and the sample export gives the same 14 events.

**6. An interrupted simulation was reused without its event logs.** Medium (latent). Fixed.
- **Where.** `src/ghostlows/sim/run.py:93,150` and `scripts/package_cohort.py:111`.
- **The bug.**
  - Resume checked only the `truth` part, which is written before `events`.
  - `load` reads each folder as a dataset, so a missing part was silently absent.
  - `make package` would have shipped the cohort without those event logs.
- **Fix.**
  - A patient-run is done only when all three parts exist.
  - Parts are written to a temporary file and then renamed.
  - Packaging refuses an incomplete cohort.
- **Tests.** `test_interrupted_patient_runs_are_simulated_again` and `test_an_incomplete_cohort_is_not_packaged`.
- **Check before packaging.** `make package` now checks this. Whether the cached cohort is complete can only be checked on the machine that holds it.

**7. The D5 reference ignored the configured refractory period.** Low (latent). Fixed.
- **Where.** `src/ghostlows/bench/pipeline.py:177,192,365` and `src/ghostlows/bench/curves.py:59,160`.
- **The bug.** `reference_sensitivity` built a fresh `TrendAlarm` with the default 30 min, while the alarms matched to it used `BenchConfig.refractory_min`.
- **Fix.** The reference now uses the configured period. Test: `test_d5_reference_fires_with_the_run_refractory_period`.
- **Effect on results.** None: no config changes the 30-min default.

**8. Provenance did not record a dirty working tree.** Low. Fixed.
- **Where.** `src/ghostlows/bench/pipeline.py:497`.
- **The gap.** `git_commit.txt` named `HEAD` even when tracked code had uncommitted changes.
- **Fix.**
  - `meta.json` now records `git_dirty`. Changes under `results/` are ignored.
  - `scripts/check_reproducible.py` warns when the flag is set.
- **Test.** `test_git_state_reports_uncommitted_changes_outside_results`.
- **Note.** The committed tables gain the field at the next `make results`.

**9. H1's CI is a percentile bootstrap of a median of 30 patients.** Low. Reported (P13).
- **Where.** `src/ghostlows/bench/analysis.py:115` and `src/ghostlows/bench/stats.py:82-107`.
- **The problem.** With 10 patients per age stratum, the bootstrap distribution of a median is discrete, and percentile intervals can under-cover.
- **Effect.** The reading is unlikely to change, since every lower bound is ≥ 0.06.

**10. Non-finite bootstrap draws are dropped without a count.** Low. Reported (P13).
- **Where.** `src/ghostlows/bench/stats.py:104` and `src/ghostlows/bench/curves.py:499-513`.
- **The problem.** A ratio with a zero denominator in a resample is discarded, so the CI is conditional on estimable draws. A13 reports the dropped share for the total ratio only, not for the compression ratio.

**11. The A11 "first reduction" is a scan without adjustment.** Low. Reported.
- **Where.** `src/ghostlows/bench/curves.py:270`.
- **The problem.** It keeps the largest of about 50 correlated retentions whose CI is below 1, so the retention it reports is optimistic. A11 is already secondary and post hoc. The wording should say it is a scan.

**12. Real-data PISA levels are not paired.** Low. Reported.
- **Where.** `src/ghostlows/faults/suite.py:287-325` and `experiments/real_data.py:173`.
- **The problem.** Each level re-draws with the same seed, and the random streams diverge after the first injected night. The simulated track uses thinning (common random numbers). Level contrasts on real data are noisier, but not biased.

**13. Limits of reproducibility.** Low. Reported.
- A fresh clone needs the 2.3-hour cohort.
- Only `rq1_pisa_sweep` was checked from a clean clone.
- The `real__*` tables carry no commit.
- The per-run figures need gitignored run outputs. The figures PR draws the main figures from the committed tables.
- `make_results_readme.py:1030` rewrites `results/tables/assumptions.md` from the current code, so its line numbers drift (known since v0.3.0, CHANGELOG).

**14. The cache key leaves out numpy and scipy.** Low. Reported.
- **Where.** `src/ghostlows/sim/run.py:63-71`.
- **Mitigation.** `uv.lock` and `uv sync --locked` pin both.

**15. Test gaps.** Low. Reported.
- `experiments/real_data.py` has no tests (`to_grid`, `reference_events`, `inject`, `semi_synthetic`).
- No test checks that `results/README.md` matches its generator. The figures PR adds one.
- The causality test uses one cut point on one synthetic run (`tests/test_alarms.py:73-107`).

**16. Docstring: the redraw share.** Info. Fixed.
- The docstring said "about 0.3% at τ = 10". The measured share is 0.6% (`src/ghostlows/faults/pisa.py:114`).

**17. Dependency pins.** Info. No action needed.
- `uv lock --check` passes and CI installs with `--locked`.
- `simglucose` is pinned to 0.2.11 and `setuptools` below 70.
- The runtime dependencies are lower bounds, as a library's should be.

## Draft amendments for DECISIONS.md

Proposals only. Nothing below is adopted or computed.

- **P10. The gap rule for CGM-referenced lows.** `reference_events` splits runs at every missing reading, although BENCHMARK.md §3 ends a run only at a gap of more than two sampling intervals.
  - *Proposal:* on real data, let a single missing reading continue a run, and end runs and events at longer gaps. Then re-run `experiments/real_data.py`.
  - *Changes:* the Weinstock reference rises to 2,918 level 1 and 1,635 level 2 lows; `real__summary.csv` and the AZT1D tables change.
  - *Alternative:* amend §3 to the rule as built.
- **P11. Specified outputs never produced.**
  - *Proposal:* report D7 detection latency, the D8 depth arms, the BENCHMARK.md §6 effective sample size and the SIMULATION.md §4.5 meal-bolus overlap share. Drop the D7 retrospective track by amendment, or run it.
  - *Changes:* adds outputs; no existing number changes.
- **P12. Duration in the τ arms.**
  - *Proposal:* state in the robustness section that τ = 20 also lengthens PISAs (median 55 vs 45 min). For a τ-only contrast, add an arm whose durations match the nominal distribution, which needs a shape that may end before full recovery.
  - *Changes:* the new arm adds rows; `robust_tau20` is unchanged.
- **P13. CI reporting.**
  - *Proposal:* add a BCa or order-statistic interval for H1 as a sensitivity analysis. Report the share of non-finite bootstrap draws behind every ratio CI, and flag shares above 5% as A13 does.
  - *Changes:* adds columns; the primary CIs are unchanged.
